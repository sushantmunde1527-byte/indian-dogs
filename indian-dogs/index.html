Native Canines of India

Discover the majestic, ancient, and deeply loyal indigenous dog breeds of the Indian subcontinent. Built by nature, refined by history.

Important Note on Native Breeds

Many of these breeds were developed for intense guarding, hunting, or surviving harsh climates. Traits like high prey drive, territoriality, and independence mean they require experienced owners, firm positive training, and early socialization. They are not recommended as mere status symbols or for first-time owners.
